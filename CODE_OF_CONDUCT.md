# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TxtPDF to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intentions and ask questions when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Please report problems by opening an issue or contacting the repository owner on GitHub.
